[SIZE=5][B]📌 Introduction[/B][/SIZE]
DaisyForGaming v1.5 is a major stability, hardware compatibility, and streaming media release for the Xiaomi Mi A2 Lite (daisy / daisy_sprout, MSM8953, Snapdragon 625).
v1.5 resolves video playback buffer stalls across Facebook and other media platforms, restores external MicroSD card hardware detection, activates the PM8953 PMIC thermal zone driver, and guarantees full storage data integrity.

[SIZE=5][B]🚀 What's New & Fixed in v1.5[/B][/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*][B]Video Playback & Media Streaming Buffer Fix:[/B]
Tuned TCP socket buffer limits (`rmem_max` and `wmem_max` to 6 MB, `tcp_rmem` to 6 MB, `tcp_wmem` to 4 MB) with BBR congestion control and `fq` packet pacing. Disabled `tcp_slow_start_after_idle` (`= 0`) and enabled `tcp_mtu_probing` (`= 1`), eliminating buffer stalls and lag during video playback across Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, and browsers.
[*][B]External SD Card Hardware Detection:[/B]
Corrected SD Card Detect GPIO polarity in `msm8953-qrd-sku3-daisy.dts` and `sakura-qrd.dtsi` (`cd-gpios = <&tlmm 133 0x0>`) with DDR50 bus speed mode. MicroSD cards now mount cleanly on system boot and in recovery without delay.
[*][B]PM8953 PMIC Thermal Zone Driver (`pm8953_tz`):[/B]
Activated the PM8953 thermal zone in device tree, providing accurate hardware thermal readings on `thermal_zone2`.
[*][B]Data Integrity & Vanilla Fsync Restored:[/B]
Disabled unsafe `CONFIG_DYNAMIC_FSYNC` and restored vanilla Linux kernel `fsync()` / `fdatasync()` system call semantics. Zero risk of zero-byte file corruption during sudden restarts or power loss.
[*][B]Retained All v1.4 Performance Enhancements:[/B]
Cold app-launch instant Zygote boost (150 ms CPU + DDR bus boost), smooth 80 ms input boost, 652 MHz idle frequency floor, eMMC 256 KB read-ahead, zero comments, zero warnings, and Magisk root preservation.
[/LIST]
