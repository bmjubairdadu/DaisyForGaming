[SIZE=5][B]📌 Introduction[/B][/SIZE]
DaisyForGaming is an optimized, high-performance custom kernel for the Xiaomi Mi A2 Lite (daisy) built with the latest Clang 21 toolchain and LLD linker.
Version 1.3 addresses all known issues from v1.2, restoring external SD card mounting, eliminating video stuttering/buffering, introducing Dynamic Fsync, and balancing gaming responsiveness with ice-cold thermal management.

[SIZE=5][B]🚀 Features & Improvements in v1.3[/B][/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*][B]External SD Card Hardware Detection Fix:[/B] Corrected SD Card Detect GPIO polarity to Active Low in Device Tree (GPIO 133). Fixed filesystem mounting with native NTFS and exFAT support.
[*][B]Video Lag & Buffering Elimination:[/B] Restored GPU memory bus persistence (force_bus_on = 1) at a 216 MHz clock floor, eliminating SurfaceFlinger starvation during 60 FPS YouTube and local playback. Scaled TCP stream socket buffers to 8 MB.
[*][B]Gaming Performance & Thermal Balance:[/B] Tuned input boost duration to 80 ms (fast responsiveness without core-pinning heat). Disabled boost-on-charging to prevent overheating during fast charging. Configured schedutil governor rate limits (500us up, 20ms down). Pinned touchscreen interrupts to CPUs 0-3 for low latency.
[*][B]Dynamic Fsync (Screen-State Aware):[/B] Accelerates storage write throughput and game asset loading while display is active, safely flushing dirty pages to NAND on suspend and shutdown.
[*][B]Wakelock Blocker & Wakeup Suppression:[/B] Suppresses rogue background Wi-Fi and Qualcomm wakeups to ensure 0% battery drain during screen-off sleep.
[*][B]Clang 21 & Clean Code:[/B] Compiled with Ubuntu Clang 21.1.8 with zero warnings. Completely clean, comment-free source code.
[*][B]Root Compatible:[/B] Seamless Magisk and FolkPatch support with skip_initramfs ramdisk retention.
[/LIST]
